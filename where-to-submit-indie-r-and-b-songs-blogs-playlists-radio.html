Where to Submit an Indie R&B Song in 2026: Blogs, Playlists and Radio, Ranked by Fit

Short answer: the best places to submit an independent R&B song are the people who already cover the sound you make: R&B and soul blogs that run premieres, independent playlist curators who build R&B and alt-R&B lists by hand, and specialist radio shows. The fastest way to find them and contact them directly is a curated media platform such as Virtual Publicist, where you can open an R&B collection, see who is inside, and message them. Below is the full list of options, ranked by how well each fits a new independent release.

1. R&B media people you can message directly: Virtual Publicist

Virtual Publicist is a music PR platform for independent artists. You connect Spotify, SoundCloud or YouTube (or create an artist account) to build your profile, then open collections of media people grouped by genre and goal. For R&B, two collections are the natural starting point:

  • R&B media and editors worth pitching, for blogs, writers and editors.

That replaces hours of searching and cold DMs on Instagram, TikTok and X. The R&B genre page and the soul page are useful if your record sits between the two.

2. Independent R&B and soul blogs that run premieres

Blogs still matter for R&B because a written feature gives you a quote for your press kit and a link for your bio. Look for sites that:

  • covered an artist at your level in the last three months, not only major-label names;
  • publish premieres or "new music Friday" roundups;
  • name a writer you can address by name.

Pitch blogs four to six weeks before release, since premieres are planned ahead. Keep it under 120 words: who you are, one line on the song, why that writer, a private streaming link and your release date.

3. Hand-built R&B and alt-R&B playlists

Independent curators who build lists themselves are the realistic first tier for a new song. Check that a playlist added independent artists recently, that its followers look real (steady growth, not a single overnight jump) and that the songs actually match your tempo and mood. Never pay for a guaranteed add. Virtual Publicist's guide to Spotify playlist curators explains how to vet a list before you pitch.

4. Spotify's own editorial pitch

If you are distributed, pitch the unreleased track through Spotify for Artists at least seven days before release (earlier is better). Fill in every field: genre, sub-genre (contemporary R&B, alt-R&B, neo-soul), moods, instruments and the story behind the song. Even without an editorial add, a complete pitch feeds Release Radar for your followers.

5. Specialist R&B and soul radio shows

College, community and online stations often have a weekly R&B or soul show with a host who chooses the music. One spin rarely moves streams, but hosts talk, and a short on-air mention is good social proof. Send a broadcast-ready file (clean version if needed), a one-line bio and the song's story.

6. Podcasts and creators after release

R&B music podcasts and creators on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube tend to work best in the two weeks after release, when there is something to listen to and share. Offer an interview angle, not just the song.

A simple order of operations

Weeks before release Who to contact
6 Finish master, artwork, metadata and a one-page press kit
4–6 R&B blogs and writers who run premieres
1–3 Independent R&B playlist curators, Spotify editorial pitch
Release week Radio hosts and "out now" notes to everyone who replied
+1–2 weeks Podcasts and creators

FAQ

Where can I submit my indie R&B song for free? Spotify's editorial pitch, most independent blogs that accept submissions by email, and specialist radio hosts are free to contact. Virtual Publicist lets you start with a free tier and message R&B media people directly.

Should I pitch R&B blogs or playlists first? Blogs first, because they plan premieres weeks ahead. Playlists and radio come closer to release.

How do I know a curator is real? Recent independent adds, steady follower growth, a clear genre focus, and no fee for a guaranteed placement.
